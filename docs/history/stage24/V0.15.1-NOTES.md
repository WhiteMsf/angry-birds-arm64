# v0.15.1 — deterministic bird-contact microprobe

v0.15 moved RedBird_4 to a position 1.95 units left of WoodBlock2_6 and gave
it +8 units/s velocity. No BeginContact was observed.

The final bird position also showed that the original Lua/slingshot path later
moves the bird again, but that alone does not explain why the immediately
following Box2D Step produced no contact.

v0.15.1 changes only the diagnostic microprobe:

- logs RedBird_4 Box2D body type;
- starts RedBird_4 with a small intentional overlap against WoodBlock2_6
  (center offset 1.60; approximate touching distance 1.793);
- keeps +8 units/s horizontal velocity;
- logs actual body position/velocity immediately after SetTransform;
- logs position/velocity and total BeginContact count immediately after the
  fixed 1/30 Step.

No birdCollision damage/scoring semantics are implemented or guessed.

If the overlapped dynamic body still produces no contact, the new log will
tell us that the remaining boundary is not simple geometry/timing and we can
inspect body activation/filter/joint state directly.
