# v0.23.5 / Stage 22.5 — Pig fidelity audit

## Question

Why does `SmallPiglette_7` in Level1 appear to die so easily?

The current ARM64 port has high-confidence recovery of the ordinary native
damage rule, but the exact Rovio Box2D fork/world-construction parameters are
not yet fully certified. This stage audits physics before changing gameplay.

## External research conclusion

Independent Angry Birds simulation research is unusually relevant here:
pigs are empirically fragile, and small physics/simulation errors can produce
large differences in pig destruction. Researchers also observed simulations
that over-damaged scenes when reconstructed structures were slightly unstable.

The original Poached Eggs 1-1 is genuinely designed as a very easy one-shot
collapse: one Red into the long horizontal wood beneath the pig can clear it.
Pig fragility itself is therefore expected; idle/pre-impact damage is the
suspicious part.

## Audit design

This is a **10-second idle control**.

The original Lua update loop runs. The recovered 30 Hz Box2D schedule runs.
Recovered collision damage remains enabled. But the harness never publishes a
single LBUTTON press, drag or release and never calls `ApplyImpulse`.

For every `BeginContact` involving `SmallPiglette_7`, the ARM64 runtime records:

- contact frame and other object;
- positions and linear/angular velocities;
- masses/body types/awake state;
- relative velocity and speed;
- momentum-delta vector;
- exact recovered native metric:
  `0.1 * |mA*vA - mB*vB|`;
- contact point and normal;
- normal/tangent relative-speed components;
- fixture density/friction/restitution;
- pig strength/defence before;
- predicted ordinary damage;
- pig strength after / actual damage / destruction;
- correlated Box2D solver normal/tangent impulse from `PostSolve`.

Baseline telemetry also records the pig and its initial support
`WoodBlock2_1`, including exact fixture-derived vertical surface gap.

## Critical interpretation rule

If the pig loses health in this idle control, **do not compensate by increasing
pig strength or defence**.

That would hide the discrepancy. The next audit target becomes world/body
construction fidelity: gravity, exact Box2D fork/revision, damping, sleep,
contact tolerances and other recovered native creation parameters.

The current reconstruction uses world gravity `(0, 10)`. Stage 22.5 reports it
explicitly as **not yet independently verified against the Rovio ARMv7 world
constructor**.
