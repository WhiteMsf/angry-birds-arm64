# v0.23.6 — Stage 22.6: polygon-skin fidelity A/B

## Why this stage exists

Stage 22.5 proved a real fidelity problem without touching the controls:

- no press / drag / release / `ApplyImpulse`;
- the Level1 SmallPiglette still lost ~38.4% of its strength;
- the single damaging contact was the pig landing on `WoodBlock2_1`;
- the contact arrived with a relative speed around 3.776 and native damage
  metric around 2.535.

That means the pig's low `strength=4` is not, by itself, the next thing to
change. We need to explain why the reconstructed structure creates that
landing impact while idle.

## Static ARMv7 evidence

The earlier `libangrybirds.so` reconstruction of the GameLua shape creation
routines found an easily missed detail:

```text
polygon shape radius = 0.1f
```

The recovered note applies to the box/polygon creation routines.

Our current ARM64 `createBox()` had only:

```cpp
b2PolygonShape shape;
shape.SetAsBox(width * 0.5f, height * 0.5f);
```

so the reference Box2D constructor kept its upstream polygon-skin radius.

In stock Box2D the polygon skin is tied to `b2_polygonRadius`, normally
`2 * b2_linearSlop` (0.01 when linear slop is 0.005). The recovered Rovio
value is therefore about ten times larger.

## Why Level1 is a particularly sensitive test

Stage 22.5 measured the nominal geometric vertical surface gap between
`SmallPiglette_7` and `WoodBlock2_1` as only about:

```text
0.053758621
```

A 0.01 polygon skin does not bridge that nominal gap. A 0.1 skin exceeds it.

This does **not** prove the Box2D solver will behave identically merely from
the arithmetic above; polygon radius participates in the collision proxy and
contact/stacking machinery. That is why Stage 22.6 is an A/B runtime test
instead of silently patching production behavior.

## Experimental design

The same ARM64 audit binary runs twice:

### A — upstream-default
`b2PolygonShape::SetAsBox()` keeps its reference Box2D default radius.

### B — recovered-armv7-0.1
Immediately after `SetAsBox()`:

```cpp
shape.m_radius = 0.1f;
```

Nothing else changes:

- gravity remains `(0, 10)`;
- fixed step remains `1/30`;
- solver iterations remain `10/10`;
- Level1 positions remain original;
- density/friction/restitution remain original recovered values;
- pig strength/defence remain `4/1`;
- damage metric remains the recovered ARMv7 formula;
- there is zero player input.

## Interpretation

The PowerShell driver automatically compares both reports.

- **Default damages, recovered 0.1 does not:** strong evidence that the missing
  Rovio polygon skin caused the suspicious idle pig damage.
- **Recovered 0.1 reduces but does not eliminate damage:** skin radius matters,
  but another world/solver fidelity difference remains.
- **No meaningful change:** audit gravity/world construction/Rovio Box2D fork
  behavior next.

This stage is fidelity work, not gameplay balancing. Do not buff the pig.
