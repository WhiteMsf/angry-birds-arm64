# v0.24.8

## Stage 23.6A

Adds `stage23_6a_renderobject_interpolation` and `build-stage23-6a-renderobject-interpolation-android-arm64.ps1`.

This revision reconstructs the original ARMv7 `GameLua::update(float)` RenderObjectData two-slot transform history and fixed-step interpolation instead of publishing raw Box2D transforms directly to Lua/rendering.

Recovered details implemented:

- two `(x,y,angle)` history slots;
- global 0/1 history index;
- awake-body capture gate (`e_awakeFlag = 0x0002` in the pinned Box2D 2.1.2-era layout);
- catch-up rule that skips intermediate history snapshots while accumulator remains above two fixed steps;
- `alpha = accumulator / (1/30)`;
- previous/current linear interpolation for x/y;
- shortest-path angular interpolation across pi;
- `setPosition` and `setRotation` force both history slots plus published transform, matching the ARMv7 setters;
- Lua physics sync and GLES snapshots consume the published interpolated transform.
