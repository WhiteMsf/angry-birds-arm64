## Runtime evidence carried forward

The v0.26.135 diagnostics closed the primary distance-joint fidelity failure. Danger Above 6-15 creates nine `type=1` / `coordType=2` distance joints between pig bodies and static balloon bodies on each load. With the reconstructed joints present, score remains zero until the first real shot instead of climbing by ~55k before input. A restart recreates all nine joints cleanly. Mighty Hoax 5-11 creates two `type=1` / `coordType=0` joints, including the suspended LightBlock12_1↔WoodBlock2_1 structure that originally exposed the missing contract. The 0.26.135 runtime created 20 native distance joints total across 6-15 load/restart and 5-11, produced no joint/Lua/fatal errors, and body destruction exercised automatic joint-record cleanup. A direct Lua `destroyJoint(name)` hit was not observed in that capture, so that wrapper remains statically reconstructed rather than independently runtime-hit.

## This stage

- Reconstruct `setMaxTranslation(float)` exactly from ARMv7: write the input to max translation and input² to its squared companion. Stock pinned Box2D 2.1.2 defines that pair as macros (`2.0f` / its square), so a build-time patch converts only those two definitions into writable `float32` globals, matching the mutability proven by the shipping ARMv7 setter. The original 2.0/4.0 defaults are retained until Lua calls the setter.
- Preserve `setLevelLimits(float,float,float,float)` as state-only: ARMv7 converts only arg1 and arg3 to integers and stores them at GameLua+0x24c/+0x250. No body is removed, clamped, slept, or teleported yet.
- Add passive `OUTSIDE_ENTER`, `OUTSIDE_EXIT`, and `OUTSIDE_SUMMARY` telemetry for active dynamic bodies crossing those candidate horizontal limits.
- Scan the complete ARMv7 disassembly for every GameLua method referencing +0x24c/+0x250, so the next implementation is owned by the real consumer rather than a guessed offscreen rule.
- Keep `setGameOn(bool)` observe-only. Its ARMv7 body is preserved: GameLua+0x20 is the constructor-supplied `framework::App*`; setGameOn dereferences App+4 and invokes virtual slot +0x18 with the boolean negated.

No level-specific branches are introduced.
