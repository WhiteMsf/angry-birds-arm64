# v0.26.138 — Stage 24.44.1 — Level-limit / update consumer capture

## Why this stage exists

The first successful Stage24.44.0a runtime produced the exact long-wait failure
shape the bounds audit was designed to catch. In Poached Eggs 1-1,
`setLevelLimits(-66.848801, -10000, 104.954346, 20)` mirrored the two ARMv7
stored integers `minX=-66` and `maxX=104`. `RedBird_4` crossed the right limit at
frame 1098 at x=104.447426 with high linear/angular velocity and remained beyond
the limit until its ordinary Lua bird-removal path destroyed the body at frame
2264. That is 1166 frames, about 19.43 seconds. During the outside interval the
level repeatedly reported `hasMovingObjects=true`, and completion did not gate
until after the lingering birds were removed.

This is strong runtime correlation, but it still does not prove what the
original C++ consumer of GameLua+0x24c/+0x250 did. It may exclude outside bodies
from motion gates, remove/cull them, or use the fields elsewhere. Do not guess.

## Stage24.44.1

- Build-time `stage24441_level_limit_update_consumer_contract.py` disassembles
  the complete ARMv7 `GameLua::update(float)` and every named `GameLua::` symbol
  with an explicit bracketed +0x24c/+0x250 field access.
- Large context windows around every access are preserved in
  `stage24.44.1-level-limit-update-consumer-contract.txt`.
- Runtime keeps the current `setLevelLimits` implementation state-only.
- `OUTSIDE_CLASSIFY` records exact speed²/angular velocity, Lua-world presence,
  flag87, and whether an outside body contributes to the already-proven
  `hasMovingObjects` / zero-tolerance gates.
- `SHADOW` computes those same recovered gates with out-of-bounds bodies omitted
  but never publishes the shadow values back to Lua.
- `REMOVE_OBJECT_WHILE_OUTSIDE` records the final state when untouched Lua
  eventually removes a body that is still beyond the native limits.
- The diagnostic bundle now explicitly includes all Stage24.44.0/24.44.1 static
  and runtime reports; the prior collector accidentally generated the new
  reports but omitted them from the ZIP filename list.

No clamp, DestroyBody, SetAwake, SetActive, teleport, velocity mutation, forced
completion, or per-level/per-bird branch is introduced.
