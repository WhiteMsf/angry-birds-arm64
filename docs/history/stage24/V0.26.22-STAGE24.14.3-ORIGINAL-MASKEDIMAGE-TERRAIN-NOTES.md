# v0.26.22 — Stage 24.14.3 — Original MaskedImage terrain

Scope: first visual reconstruction of the exact renderer boundary introduced by visual level 1-2 (`Level57.lua`).

## Evidence carried forward

The original ARMv7 binary retains `MaskedImage::add(float,float,gr::Image*,game::Sprite*)` and `MaskedImage::render()`.
The producer appends six vertices per accepted mask (two triangles) into three parallel arrays: position, fill UV, and mask UV. `render()` uploads position + two texture-coordinate layers, binds the fill image and mask-sheet image, draws, then clears the transient arrays.

The live Level57 contract proves all 15 current MaskedImage objects use:

- fill texture: `INGAME_THEME_GROUND_1`
- mask sheet: `INGAME_BLOCKS_1.pvr`
- mask sprites: `GROUND_BLOCK_03`, `GROUND_BLOCK_04`, `GROUND_BLOCK_05`

## Reconstruction in this revision

- stages the user's untouched original `INGAME_THEME_GROUND_1.pvr` (direct file or one-file `.pvr.zip` wrapper);
- parses/uploads it through the already recovered RGBA4444 PVR path;
- queues static MaskedImage objects during pass0 and flushes once before dynamic pass1, preserving the recovered draw boundary;
- emits six triangle vertices per visible object in the original A/B/C, C/B/D topology;
- computes mask UVs from the exact sprite rect in `INGAME_BLOCKS_1.pvr`;
- computes fill UVs from world-space corner positions divided by the full fill-image dimensions, matching the simplified full-image form of the recovered ARMv7 producer calculation;
- uses GLES1 multitexture: unit0 theme fill (REPEAT), unit1 sprite mask (CLAMP_TO_EDGE), RGBA modulation;
- restores the previous single-texture state after the batch;
- retains a strict failure for any masked fill texture or mask sheet outside the recovered Level57 contract.

No physics, Lua, camera, menu scaling, font, progression, collision, or ordinary sprite semantics are changed.
